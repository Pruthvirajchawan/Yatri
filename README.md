# GlobeTrotter

An intelligent travel planning platform designed around realistic, personalized and collaborative trip decisions.

---

### 1. Problem

Traditional travel planning forces users to manually balance competing constraints:

- Destinations
- Time
- Transport
- Activities
- Budget
- Group preferences

The fundamental issue is not simply creating an itinerary. The deeper challenge is making high-quality, practical travel decisions under real-world constraints.

---

### 2. Vision

GlobeTrotter aims to help travelers understand the trade-offs behind every trip decision and continuously refine their plan into its most realistic, enjoyable version.

---

### 3. Core Product Direction

The platform is designed around several key concepts (*planned product concepts*):

- **Experience Budget**: Accounting for physical energy and cognitive capacity alongside financial spend.
- **Trip Debt**: Preventing hidden fatigue caused by repeated tight compromises.
- **Regret Map**: Prioritizing core experiences a traveler would most regret missing.
- **Group Equity**: Balancing preferences fairly rather than letting the loudest voice dominate.
- **Trade-Off Engine**: Surfacing exact gains and sacrifices for every routing or schedule adjustment.
- **Travel DNA**: Learning dynamically from behavioral choices over time.
- **Domino Effect Simulator**: Modeling downstream impacts on schedule, budget, and logistics when one element shifts.
